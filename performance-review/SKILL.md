# Performance Review

Run on the first business day for the closed prior month when the analytics
revision or period is new. Include top/bottom performers, content pillars,
formats, month-over-month movement, quality scores, sample size, coverage, and
limitations. Insufficient data may produce a data-insufficient status but must
not produce a strong strategy or consume an LLM. Same project/month/revision
is idempotent; manual reruns name the month explicitly.
