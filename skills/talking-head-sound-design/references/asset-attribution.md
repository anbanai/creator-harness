## Asset source and rights

The six bundled OGG effects are CC0 1.0 files from the `sound-effects` skill in [Barty-Bart/motion-graphics](https://github.com/Barty-Bart/motion-graphics), pinned at commit `3d83d987d658601d8a1269eb691622bdcd427335`. They were selected from its default kit. Their creators, source pages, asset hashes, and optional attribution are recorded in `assets/sfx/manifest.json`.

The upstream repository describes these assets as CC0 and permits redistribution. The upstream sound-pass implementation is not copied. Anban's local renderer is an independent implementation that keeps the useful cue, mix, and measurement workflow while excluding network downloads and unneeded music processing.
